# Code of conduct

## In short

Be decent. Argue about the code, not about the person.

## What we expect

- Assume the other person is competent and acting in good faith.
- Disagree with evidence: a measurement, a failing test, a line of code.
- Accept that "I do not know" is a complete and respectable answer.
- Keep other people's data out of issues, pull requests and test fixtures.

## What is not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or demeaning comments about anyone.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of a discussion.

## Enforcement

Report a problem to **kevin.borger@gjk-partners.nl**. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that
break these rules, and may block a contributor from the project.

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and anywhere
someone represents the project in public.
